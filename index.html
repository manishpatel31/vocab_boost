Fill in the blank

          
Pulls from each word's SSC-style sentence, blanks out the term, and asks you to pick which word fits — same format as an SSC vocabulary question. Uses your current Library filters as the word pool.

          
        

        

          
Confusable pairs (false friends)

          
Drills you on words your register has flagged as commonly confused with another (e.g. affect/effect) — the kind of trap SSC papers love. Needs both words of a pair to be in your library.

          
        

        

          
Spelling challenge

          
Hear a word aloud and prove you can spell it, three ways. Draws from every vocabulary word in your library (idioms and phrases are skipped) — independent of any Library search or filters you've set.

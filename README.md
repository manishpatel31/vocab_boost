# पाठShala — SSC study app

A free, long-term study app for all SSC exams (CGL Tier 1 & 2, CHSL, CPO, Steno).
Vocabulary, General Studies and Maths in one place, with one daily goal, one streak and progress that syncs across devices.

**Live site:** https://manishpatel31.github.io/vocab_boost/

## What's inside

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Today's goal, streak, revision due, and every subject as a card |
| **Vocab** | Word of the day, all words, Spelling Bank, Roots, Add words, Present, AOD, Audio |
| **Practice** | Today's revision, Mistakes book, Mixed mock (English + Maths + Reasoning + GS), Weak spots, Vocab quiz, Revision, Exam tomorrow |
| **Progress** | Study points, streak, per-subject progress |
| **Search** | One box that searches lessons and notes across every mini app |

**Today's revision** (Home → Waiting for you, and Practice across subjects) puts everything due in one list. Every question you once got wrong — in a mini app, the Mistakes book or a mock — joins a spaced-review queue and comes back after 1, 3, 7, 15 and 30 days. A right answer moves it to the next gap, a wrong one starts it again the next day, and a right answer after the 30-day gap retires it. The same screen lists apps due for revision (study tracker), vocab words due back and today's tasks, with the questions due over the next seven days. The queue is saved as `shabdReview` and synced with the account like the mini apps.

**Mixed mock** has one-tap presets (full Tier 1: 100 questions in 60 minutes; half paper; GS only) and, after every mock, the topics where marks were lost. **Weak spots** can build a timed 25-question mock from your weakest topics (wrong answers first, then questions not yet tried).
| `study-search.json` | Lesson text index used by Search |
| `wallpaper.html` | Live desktop wallpaper (works with Lively Wallpaper) showing words and GS/Maths fact cards |
| `sw.js` | Service worker: offline use and caching |
| `manifest.json`, `*.png` | Install-to-home-screen settings and icons |

> **Please don't rename, move or delete these files.** The app, the offline cache, search and the Android companion all look for them at these exact names and paths.

## Features

- Phone and PC layouts, Paper and Indigo themes, each with Light / Dark / Auto
- Opens instantly after the first visit, even on slow mobile data: the app, its libraries and fonts load from the saved copy and update in the background (a “new version is ready” bar offers a reload); every mini app, the question bank and search are saved for offline use once the app is idle; installable on a phone
- Progress saved to your account (Firebase) and synced across devices
- One unified daily goal and streak across vocab and all study apps
- Study tracker: every visit to a mini app is saved as a session (active time, questions answered, score), with score trends, time estimates, last visited / studied / completed dates and a reminder to revise each app every 15 days (10–30, adjustable)
- Plan tab (next to Practice): a month calendar of your study plans, central government gazetted holidays (2026–27) and your exams (add as many as you like, each with a name and date; the nearest drives the home countdown), with the tasks of the day you tap listed below it, grouped by plan. Make a plan by picking any mini apps, the days (or “until my exam”), a start date and the time you have each day, then tell the AI about your day in plain words (“working professional, 2 hours on working days and 4 at weekends, revise the week’s lessons every Sunday”). The AI asks a quick question when something that matters is unclear (e.g. whether gazetted holidays are off for you), then shapes the schedule — minutes per weekday, rest days, holidays, which apps go first, weekly review (the week’s lessons and/or a mock) and revision days — and the app lays its real lessons out on it. You see the whole plan day by day before saving, and can ask the AI to change it (“reduce it to 25 days”, “Saturday off”). Each plan is a named group: rename it, move all its days at once (±1/±7 days or a new start date), edit it with AI, change its apps, days or time (days already gone stay as they are), or delete it. Several plans can run side by side. Lessons tick themselves when finished in their app; a day's tasks can be sent to Tasks
- Tasks: a to-do list — type a task and press Enter; each task needs 1, 2 or 3 ticks to finish (do it, then revise it). Shortcuts for #tags, ! important and due dates (today, tomorrow, a weekday), app names and chapter names (or "formula book chapter 3") become links to the app and to that chapter, editing a task re-reads its shortcuts, revision-due apps are suggested, a 25-minute focus timer adds a tick when it ends, Today / Upcoming / Done lists, undo delete, synced across devices
- Deep links: Search results open the exact lesson
- AI tutor (optional, off by default): turn it on under Account → AI tutor and paste your own free API key (Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter). Ask any SSC doubt in Explain, Solve, Vocab, PYQs, Quiz me, Mock test, Brainstorm or My weak spots mode, with replies in English, Hinglish or Hindi. Pick how the answer should look (Quick answer, Step by step, In detail, Detail + examples, From basics, Revision notes, Tricks only); under each answer, one tap gets PYQs, practice questions or a timed 20-question mock on that topic, a simpler version, more examples, memory tricks, revision notes or a related topic. Answers are framed for SSC CGL and use the app's own lesson notes, questions and words when they match, linking to the lesson; Gemini can also search Google for current affairs. The AI is also built into the app: a ✨ Ask AI button in every mini app knows which lesson is open (and asks about any text you select), every app card on Home has an ✨ AI button that analyses your progress in that app (important topics, weightage, a mock from that app's questions, a plan, PYQs, weak topics, last-minute revision), and answered questions in Practice, the Mistakes book and mock results have “Ask AI why”. The model is chosen automatically: Auto ranks every model the key can use, takes the best one that answers, and skips models that failed recently. With keys for more than one service (Use all my saved keys, on by default), each question goes to the service best suited to it — Gemini for current affairs, Hindi, maths and long explanations; Groq for quick answers, words and quiz questions — and the next service answers when one is busy or out of free limit. Each answer shows the service and model that wrote it. The key stays on that device and requests go straight from the browser to the AI company. Quiz me uses the app's question bank when it covers the topic, otherwise the AI writes new questions
- Mini app builder prompt (Account → Admin): pick a subject, the exams, the app it is for (or a new one) and a few options, and copy a prompt for ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude. Paste it with a PDF, PPT, notes or a video transcript attached, and the AI writes every lesson, story, fact table, trick, trap, question (with past-paper tags it can verify), revision card, tile and Lab game idea in the plain-text format `tools/` builds apps from. For an existing app the prompt lists that app's lessons, so new material fits around them. "Continue" and "check your work" prompts handle long sources
- Companion Android app "Vocab Lock" shows words and GS/Maths facts on the lock screen

## Adding a new mini app

1. Build the page as `study-<name>.html` in the same format as the existing ones (lessons, quick-revise, practice, progress synced to the account).
2. Add its questions and lesson text to `study-bank.json` and `study-search.json`.
3. Register it in `index.html` (the `STUDY` list and name map) so it gets a card on Home.
4. Add the file to the `WARM` list in `sw.js` and bump `VERSION` (e.g. `shabd-v15` → `shabd-v16`) so phones pick up the change.
5. Commit and push. GitHub Pages updates in a minute or two.

## Hosting & notes

- Hosted on GitHub Pages straight from the `main` branch root.
- The Firebase config inside `index.html` is meant to be public. Data is protected by Firestore security rules, not by hiding the config.
- The Android project lives in a separate **private** repository because it contains the app signing key.
